# Supporting knowledge through OPENFRUIT

OPENFRUIT is free software licensed under the GNU General Public License,
version 2 or (at your option) any later version. You may use, study, modify
and distribute it, including commercially, under the terms of that license.

We believe knowledge belongs to everyone. If OPENFRUIT creates commercial
value for you, we ask you to share part of it with people who need it most.

## Who we ask

- Companies offering commercial build, CI/CD or app-delivery services to
  third parties in which OPENFRUIT runs, for example hosted iOS build
  runners, managed pipelines or build-as-a-service platforms.
- Organizations that rely on OPENFRUIT in their own commercial products and
  have the means to contribute.

Individuals, students, researchers, nonprofits and open source projects are
not asked for anything.

## Where to donate

Please donate directly to one of:

- **Save the Children**, or
- **a scientific institute conducting medical research**, preferably one
  based in Southern Italy, such as a public research hospital (IRCCS) or a
  public or nonprofit biomedical research centre.

Choose an organization that publishes its financial statements and receives
donations through its official channels. Donations go directly to the
organization you choose, never through the OPENFRUIT maintainers.

## How much

An amount proportionate to the value OPENFRUIT provides to you, decided by
you.

This request is not part of the license and does not restrict any right
granted by the GNU General Public License. You may use OPENFRUIT whether or
not you donate.
